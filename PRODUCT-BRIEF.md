Sill Garden — Product brief
Date: 2026-08-13

============================================================
WHAT IT IS
============================================================
Sill Garden is a niche affiliate content site for people who grow herbs and greens on a windowsill or kitchen counter — especially in apartments and rentals (no yard, limited space, landlord constraints).

Brand: Sill Garden
Domain (planned): sillgarden.com
Tagline: Grow on your sill — small space, real harvests.
Repo: https://github.com/nivco/sill-garden
Stack: Astro static site → Cloudflare Pages (deploy pending CF login / Pages connect)

It is NOT:
- A SaaS product
- A marketplace / rental of garden space
- Cannabis / grow-tent content
- An outdoor landscaping site

============================================================
PROBLEM + AUDIENCE
============================================================
Apartment dwellers want fresh herbs/greens but have:
- Tiny counters / sills
- Weak natural light
- Noise sensitivity (studios)
- Deposit risk (water, drilling)

They search for countertop hydro kits, AeroGarden vs Click & Grow, quiet setups, and beginner herb guides — then buy on Amazon.

============================================================

An SEO guide site with three content clusters:

3) Setup & fixes — light schedules, landlord-safe placement, noise

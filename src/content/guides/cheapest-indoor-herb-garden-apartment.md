## Decision rules (read once)

1. **Daylight first** — if a hand casts a sharp shadow at noon on the sill, soil pots win. Soft shadow → clip LED before any kit.
2. **Tray before toys** — a waterproof boot tray protects the deposit more than a fourth seed packet.
3. **Homemade ≠ free** — Kratky needs nutrient + light honesty. Cheap jars with no light still die.
4. **Quiet beats capacity in studios** — if the bed shares the room, skip pump kits until you sleep through a harvest.
5. **Upgrade only after one success** — Click & Grow / Harvest after you know which herbs you actually eat weekly.

## Lane A: windowsill soil pots (cheap / natural / simple)

1. Three 4–6 inch pots with saucers.
2. Light potting mix (not backyard dirt).
3. Mint, chives, and parsley first (see [low-light herb picks](/guides/best-low-light-herbs-apartment/) if the sill is weak).
4. One waterproof tray under everything.

This is the cheapest path when daylight is honest. It fails when the window is a north-facing alley and you insist on basil without a light.

**Natural lane tip:** “Natural” on Google usually means soil + seed, not a marketing claim. Skip miracle sprays; keep mix airy and water when the top inch dries.

## Lane B: mason-jar Kratky (homemade / quiet / compact)

## Waterproof + lightweight (deposit-safe)

- Use a continuous boot tray under the whole cluster — not three tiny saucers that overflow into each other.
- Prefer plastic or glazed pots you can empty into the sink; heavy ceramic is fine until you move apartments.
- Wipe condensation on cold windows in winter so “waterproof” gear still fails on mold, not just leaks.
- Never put a reservoir on bare wood furniture.

## Do not cheap out on these

- A waterproof tray
- Drainage holes (or a clear Kratky plan)
- One decent clip light before a third round of dead basil
- A surge-protected outlet strip
- Seeds or starters for herbs you already cook (mint/parsley before exotic basil cultivars)

## FAQ

**Best budget herb garden under 50?**  
Soil pots + waterproof tray on a bright sill. Add a clip LED only if shadows are soft at noon. Skip branded pods until DIY works once.

**Best cheap herb garden under 50?**  
Same as budget: pots, mix, mint/chives/parsley, tray. Cheap fails when you buy a tower “deal” that needs counter depth and a pump you hate.

**Best homemade herb garden under 50?**  
Kratky mason jars or soil pots — both are homemade. Jars win for silence; soil wins if you already own pots. See [Homemade Kratky Kit](/kits/homemade-kratky/).

**Best compact herb garden under 50?**  
Three small pots on one tray, or a 3-pod wick kit later. Avoid 12-pod towers marketed as “compact.”

**Best quiet herb garden under 50?**  
Kratky jars or Click & Grow (wick, no pump). AeroGarden-class pumps are fine in a separate kitchen, not beside a bed.

**Best simple herb garden under 50?**  
Two herbs you cook weekly, one tray, one light decision. Complexity is how $40 projects become $120 clutter.

**Best natural herb garden under 50?**  
Soil + seed + daylight. “Natural” is not a product category — it is skipping unnecessary gadgets until light and water are honest.

**Best healthy herb garden under 50?**  
Healthy = enough light, clean water, and harvest before plants bolt. Price tags labeled “healthy” do not fix a dark north sill.

**Best quick herb garden under 50?**  
Buy starter plants, not seed, for mint and parsley. Seed is cheaper but slower; kits still need germination time.

**Best lightweight herb garden under 50?**  
Plastic pots + tray + clip LED. You should be able to move the whole setup in one trip to the sink.

**Best waterproof herb garden under 50?**  
Waterproof means the **furniture** stays dry: continuous boot tray, no overfilled reservoirs before travel, wipe window condensation. The plants themselves do not need a sealed aquarium.

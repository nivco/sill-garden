You do not need a $100 kit to start. The cheapest indoor herb garden is the one that matches your light and stops water damage — not the one with the most pods.

![Budget pots ready for seed starting](/images/inline-pots.jpg)

## What “under $50” actually buys

| Lane | Typical spend | Best when |
|------|---------------|-----------|
| Soil pots on a bright sill | $20–40 | You already get decent daylight |
| Mason-jar Kratky + tray | $25–45 | You want silence and a learning project |
| Pots + clip-on LED | $40–70 | The window is dim (budget stretches) |
| Used / small 3-pod kit | $50–90 | You value timers more than DIY |

Electricity for a small clip light is usually cents per day. The expensive line item later is branded pods, not the first hardware.

## Lane A: windowsill soil pots

1. Three 4–6 inch pots with saucers.
2. Light potting mix.
3. Mint, chives, and parsley (see [low-light herb picks](/guides/best-low-light-herbs-apartment/) if the sill is weak).
4. One waterproof tray under everything.

This is the cheapest path when daylight is honest. It fails when the window is a north-facing alley and you insist on basil without a light.

## Lane B: mason-jar Kratky

A jar, net pot, and nutrient solution can grow lettuce or basil quietly. Add a drip tray. Pair with a clip light if the counter is away from the window. Full rental checklist: [landlord-safe setup](/guides/landlord-safe-indoor-garden-setup/).

## Lane C: small kit (upgrade)

Buy a Click & Grow–class 3-pod unit when you keep forgetting watering, your sill never cooperates, or you want a gift that works on day one. Compare options in [best countertop gardens for apartments](/guides/best-countertop-garden-apartments/).

![Chives for a cheap first harvest](/images/inline-chives.jpg)

## Do not cheap out on these

- A waterproof tray
- Drainage holes (or a clear Kratky plan)
- One decent clip light before a third round of dead basil
- A surge-protected outlet strip

## 30-day shopping list

1. Tray + three pots or a jar kit
2. Clip-on LED if the sill fails the “strong shadow” test
3. Seeds or starter plants for mint/chives first
4. Optional: Click & Grow Smart Garden 3 when DIY fatigue hits

> **Key takeaway**
> Spend first on light and spill protection. Pods and bigger kits come after you know which herbs you actually eat.

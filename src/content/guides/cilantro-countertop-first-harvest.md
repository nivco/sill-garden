Cilantro is the herb people love in salsa and the plant that disappoints them indoors. It is not hard — it is **impatient**. Treat it as a succession crop, not a permanent bush.

![Cilantro leaves close up — cut young before flower spikes](/images/inline-cilantro-alt.jpg)

## Why cilantro frustrates apartment growers

| Expectation | Reality |
|-------------|---------|
| One plant forever | Bolts when warm / day-length long |
| Basil-like pinching | Different plant — harvest leaves, then reseed |
| Hot LED 16 hours | Often pushes flowers fast |

If you need cilantro weekly, keep **two small pots** two weeks apart, or rotate one kit slot with fresh seed.

## Light and temperature

- Bright light, but avoid roasting the pot against a hot south window plus a full-power LED.
- On kits, follow height guidance and consider a slightly shorter light day than basil (many cooks do well around 12–14 hours).
- Cooler kitchen spots beat a shelf above the fridge.

## Setup

**Soil succession (best default):** Two 4–5 inch pots on a tray. Sow pot B when pot A is halfway to harvest. Stack: [Under-$50 Sill Kit](/kits/under-50/).

**Kit slot:** Fine for a few weeks of leaves. When a flower spike appears, harvest what you can and reseed the pod — do not wait for a “comeback.”

**Smell note:** Cilantro is aromatic. If scent bothers roommates or you share a studio, grow smaller amounts or see [low-smell / pet-safe picks](/guides/pet-safe-low-smell-apartment-herbs/).

## First harvest

Cut outer stems or snip leaf clusters when plants are a few inches tall. Young leaves taste brighter. Once you see a central flower stalk, flavor turns soapy/harsh for many people — harvest remaining leaves and start the next pot.

## Common failures

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Tall thin stalk, few leaves | Bolting / heat / long days | Cooler spot; shorter light; reseed |
| Yellow lower leaves | Age / water swing | Even moisture; succession sow |
| “Soapy” taste | Genetics + mature leaves | Harvest younger; try a different seed lot |
| No germination | Old seed / too deep | Fresh seed; barely cover |

## Coriander seeds

If plants bolt, you can let a few ripen for coriander spice — useful, but that is a different goal than leafy cilantro. Most apartment cooks should reseed for leaves instead.

> **Key takeaway**  
> Cilantro is a relay race. Cooler light, young harvests, and a second pot beat nursing one bolting stem.

Search demand for **Click and Grow vs Auk** and budget hydro kits is rising. The practical fork for apartments is rarely branding — it is **noise vs capacity vs refill friction**.

## Quick pick

| Need | Lean toward |
|------|-------------|
| Studio / sleep nearby | **Click & Grow** (no pump) |
| More herbs for less money | **iDOO-class** 10–12 pod |
| Known mid-tier brand | **AeroGarden** Harvest-class |
| Design-led counter piece | **Auk-class** — read recent noise/reliability reviews |

## What to compare (ignore the rest)

1. **Footprint** on a real cutting-board-sized counter  
2. **Pump noise** at night in one-room living  
3. **Pod / refill cost** after month two  
4. **Light quality** for basil (the honest test plant)

## Our apartment default

If silence matters: Click & Grow Smart Garden 3.  
If you cook a lot of herbs and can place the kit away from the bed: a well-reviewed 12-pod budget kit.  
If you want the middle brand path: AeroGarden Harvest-class.

> **Key takeaway**
> Do not buy capacity you will not harvest. Empty pods are just expensive plastic.

The sticker price of a countertop garden is not the lifetime price. **Pods** are how brands make money after you fall in love with basil.

![Fresh basil harvest that has to justify refill cost](/images/inline-refill.jpg)

## Rough math (apartment cook)

| Approach | Month 1 | Months 2–6 | Best for |
|----------|---------|------------|----------|
| Branded pods every cycle | Highest convenience | Highest cost | Gifts, zero fuss |
| One branded cycle → blanks + seeds | Medium | Much lower | Most home cooks |
| Soil pots / Kratky from day one | Lowest | Lowest | DIY comfort |

Exact dollars move with sales and seed packs — but the pattern is stable: if you harvest weekly, DIY media pays back.

## When to stay on branded pods

- You value guaranteed germination over cents per harvest  
- You are gifting the kit  
- You only run 3 pods of herbs you actually eat  

## When to switch

After one successful cycle, try blank inserts for basil or lettuce. Keep one branded pod variety you struggle to germinate yourself.

> **Key takeaway**
> Treat pods like coffee pods: great to start, expensive as a forever plan. Switch when the routine is proven.

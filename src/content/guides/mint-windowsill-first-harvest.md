Mint is forgiving, fragrant, and useful weekly — tea, yogurt, salads, garnish. It is also pushy: in a shared pot it will crowd basil. Give mint its own container.

![Young mint in a pot — trim leaf pairs, not flower spikes](/images/inline-mint-harvest.jpg)

## Setup that works in rentals

1. **Pot** — 5–7 inches wide with a saucer. Mint likes room for runners.
2. **Soil** — ordinary potting mix; do not pack it rock-hard.
3. **Light** — bright east/west sill is ideal. North sill needs a clip LED.
4. **Water** — keep evenly moist. Mint wilts dramatically, then recovers fast after a drink.

## First harvest

When stems have several leaf pairs, snip above a node. Frequent small cuts keep the plant bushy. Never strip it bald.

## Common failures

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Leggy pale stems | Weak light | Move to brighter sill or add clip LED |
| Crispy edges | Dry soil / heat near radiator | Water more evenly; move off heat |
| Tiny leaves | Hungry / rootbound | Repot one size up; light feed monthly |

> **Key takeaway**
> Mint does not need a hydroponic kit. A pot, a tray, and honest light beat another subscription pod.

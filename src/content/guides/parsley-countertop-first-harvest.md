Parsley is the weekly workhorse herb — soups, eggs, grain bowls — and it is more forgiving than basil once established. It is slower to the first cut, so people quit too early.

![Parsley on a board — harvest outer stems, leave the crown](/images/inline-parsley-alt.jpg)

## Flat-leaf vs curly

| Type | Best for | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Flat-leaf (Italian) | Cooking | Stronger flavor; preferred indoors |
| Curly | Garnish | Milder; still fine if that is what you eat |

Grow what you already buy at the store. Switching cultivars mid-hobby is how seed packets multiply unused.

## Light

- Kit LED ~14–16 hours works for parsley in a pod.
- On a sill, bright east/west is ideal; north needs a clip LED for dense leaves.
- Keep the light close enough that stems stay stocky — parsley stretches when starved of light, then tastes thin.

## Setup (soil or kit)

**Soil (recommended for weekly cooks):** 5–6 inch pot, light mix, own saucer + shared boot tray. See [Under-$50 Sill Kit](/kits/under-50/).

**Kit slot:** Fine in Harvest or Click & Grow after germination. Do not expect grocery-bunch volume from one pod — use parsley as one slot among basil/mint.

**Kratky:** Possible but soil is simpler for parsley’s longer grow. Prefer jars for basil/lettuce; see [Kratky guide](/guides/kratky-jar-herbs-apartment/).

## First harvest

Wait until stems are several inches with multiple leaf sections. Cut **outer** stems at the base; leave the center crown. Never shear the plant like a lawn.

Frequent small cuts keep production going. One giant “harvest day” shocks slow-growing parsley more than basil.

## Common failures

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Yellow older leaves | Age / inconsistent water | Harvest outer stems; water evenly |
| Leggy thin stems | Weak light | Lower LED or add clip lamp |
| Slow start | Normal for seed | Be patient 3–5 weeks; or buy a starter plant |
| Bitter / harsh | Heat stress / old leaves | Cooler spot; harvest younger outer stems |

## Pets and smell

Parsley in culinary amounts is commonly used in homes with pets, but concentrated essential oils and large chewed volumes are a different question — when in doubt, keep pots out of reach and read [pet-safe / low-smell apartment herbs](/guides/pet-safe-low-smell-apartment-herbs/). Parsley smell is mild compared with rosemary or heavy mint.

> **Key takeaway**  
> Parsley rewards patience and outer-stem cuts. Strong light matters more than a fancy nutrient schedule.

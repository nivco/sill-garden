Basil is the reason most people buy a countertop garden. It’s also the plant that shows light problems first.

## Light

- Kit LED on for ~15–16 hours is fine for basil.
- Keep the light close (follow the brand’s height guidance) so plants don’t stretch.
- On a sill without a kit, basil wants the brightest window you have — or a clip lamp.

## Pinching

When you have 2–3 sets of true leaves, pinch the tip above a leaf pair. Side shoots fill out a bushier plant. Skipping this is how you get one tall stalk.

## Harvest

Take leaves from the top down. Never strip a plant bare. Frequent small harvests beat one giant cut.

## Common failures

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Tall & pale | Not enough light | Lower LED / add lamp |
| Wilting in hydro | Low water / roots dry | Top up reservoir |
| Bitter / weak flavor | Stress or low light | Improve light; harvest younger leaves |
| White fuzz on reservoir | Algae | Block light to the water; clean |

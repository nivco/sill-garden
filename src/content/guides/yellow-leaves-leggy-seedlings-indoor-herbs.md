Yellow leaves and leggy stems are the two problems that show up in almost every apartment herb inbox. They look like “disease.” They are usually physics: not enough photons, or roots sitting wet.

![Leggy seedlings that need stronger or closer light](/images/inline-leggy.jpg)

![Stressed herb foliage that needs a light or water check](/images/inline-mold-tray.jpg)

## Leggy seedlings (tall, pale, floppy)

**Cause:** light is too weak or too far.

**Fix:**
1. Lower the LED to brand guidance (often a few inches above the canopy).
2. Run lights ~14–16 hours with a cheap timer.
3. If you are on a sill alone, add a clip-on LED — daylight through glass is weaker than product photos.

## Yellow leaves

| Pattern | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Lower leaves yellow first | Normal aging or low nitrogen | Harvest them; light feed if growth stalled |
| Whole plant yellow + wet soil | Overwatering / poor drainage | Dry down; check saucer isn’t a swamp |
| Yellow + stretch | Light starvation | Stronger / closer light first |
| Yellow tips in hydro | pH / nutrient burn | Dilute nutrients; top up with plain water once |

## Algae or fuzz on reservoirs

Block light to the water, wipe the tank, and do not overfill. A dirty reservoir is not a reason to replace the whole kit.

> **Key takeaway**
> Diagnose light and water before you spend. Most “dead basil” pages should have started with a clip light and a tray.
